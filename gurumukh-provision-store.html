Our Story

A neighbourhood shop, built on trust.

Gurumukh Provision Store began the way most good things in Lucknow do — quietly, over years of knowing our neighbours by name and remembering what every family prefers. Run by our Sikh family with the values taught to us by our elders, we treat every customer like they have walked into our home.

Small shop. Big heart.

Supermarkets have size. We have something better — relationships, reliability, and the same family at the counter every day.

The lowest prices in Lucknow

Because we buy directly from authorised distributors and keep our overheads lean, our shelf prices beat supermarkets, quick-commerce apps, and every other kirana in Alambagh. Compare any product — we're confident.

02

Genuine, sealed, top-quality goods

Every packet on our shelf comes directly from authorised distributors. No loose repacking, no shortcuts, no expired stock. What you buy is exactly what the brand sent — the highest quality, every single time.

03

We know your household

After years of serving Alambagh, we remember which atta your family prefers, which oil you buy, and when the festival season stock should come in. No algorithm can do that.
